# Tells of generated design

Each of these shows up no matter what the brief is about, which is why it reads as generated. Skip them unless the brief asks for one, in which case follow the brief.

## Palettes and looks
- Purple-to-pink or blue-to-purple gradients; gradient text; neon glows; sparkle or wand icons for "AI"; purple as the automatic AI colour.
- A warm cream background (around #F4F1EA) with a high-contrast serif and a terracotta accent (around #D97757).
- Near-black background with a single acid-green or vermilion accent.
- A faux broadsheet: hairline rules everywhere, zero radius, dense newspaper columns.

## Layout
- The SaaS card kit: every block in a same-size rounded card, one radius for all of them, an identical faint grey shadow (around rgba(0,0,0,.1)), gradient washes for decoration.
- A trio of identical feature cards; every section in the same template.
- A big number with a small label and a gradient accent as the automatic hero.
- A whole app window shrunk into a box with even margins; a product floating on a tinted plate.
- Everything at one weight; nowhere for the eye to land.
- Over-detail (every row full of real text and numbers) or the opposite, wireframe grey blobs.

## Typography and chrome
- A small, widely spaced capitals label stacked on top of each heading.
- Metadata strung together with middle dots ("Product · Design · 2026").
- Labels built as "WORD — fragment" with a spaced em dash.
- Monospace for small labels to look "technical".
- An arrow appended to every link and button.
- One word in the headline set in italic, bold or a different colour.
- 01 / 02 / 03 markers on content that is not a sequence.
- An almost-black like #0B0B0B or #111 used where black was meant.

## Imagery and icons
- Emoji used as icons or bullets in product UI.
- Mixed icon sets, mixed filled and outline glyphs, hand-drawn icons.
- Invented logos (a letter in a square).
- CSS or SVG scenes pretending to be photographs; stock-photo clichés.

## Motion
- Fade-and-slide-up on every section as you scroll.
- Hover lift and shadow on every card.
- Scattered ambient animation instead of one orchestrated moment.

## Copy
- Slogan words: elevate, seamless, unlock, supercharge, revolutionise.
- Placeholder people and companies (John Doe, Acme), fake-precise statistics.
- Buttons that say "Submit", "Learn more" or "Get started" when a specific verb exists.
